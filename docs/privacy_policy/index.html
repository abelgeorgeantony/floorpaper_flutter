Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Floorpaper ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free, open-source Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. Floorpaper does not require you to create an account, and we do not use any analytics, crash-reporting, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

Local Storage

The app stores your preferences (such as your current wallpaper configuration) and downloaded wallpaper tiles locally on your device's internal storage. This data never leaves your phone and is only used to provide the core functionality of the app—allowing you to apply tiled wallpapers and access downloaded images offline.

2. App Permissions

To function properly, Floorpaper requires the following Android permissions:

  • Internet: Used solely to fetch the catalog of available wallpaper tiles and to download the image files you select from our public GitHub repository.
  • Set Wallpaper: Used to apply the tiled images you create as your device's home screen or lock screen wallpaper.

3. Third-Party Services

The app downloads wallpaper metadata and image files from a public GitHub repository (github.com/abelgeorgeantony/tiles-archive). When your device requests these files, GitHub's servers will standardly log your IP address. We do not track these requests, nor do we associate them with any personal identity.

4. Data Security

Because we do not collect or transmit any personal data to our own servers, there is no risk of your personal data being compromised through our app. All downloaded assets and configurations are stored securely in your device's local application support directories.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
